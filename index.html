Zixuan Qi

Hi, I'm Zixuan. I'm a PhD student in mechanical engineering at Texas A&M. Most of my time goes into two small research houses on the RELLIS campus. I wrote the controller that's meant to carry one of them through a blackout on solar and battery power, I look after the data coming out of both, and lately I've been trying to get AI agents to do the boring engineering work a house needs, like checking sensors and fitting models.

I'd rather build something that runs in a real house than something that only looks good in a paper.

Projects

Getting through a blackout

Problem
When the grid goes down, a house with solar and a battery has to make its stored energy last.
What I did
Wrote the controller that decides when the heat pump and other loads run, and tested it in one of our houses.
Status
Field-tested in fall 2026, paper in progress. Part of a DOE project led by Slipstream.
Running daily

Data from two research houses

Problem
Readings from our two houses kept getting lost on the way to the database.
What I did
Took over the data system for about 300 sensor points and rebuilt how readings reach the cloud, with alerts when anything stops.
Status
Running. The field tests in both houses use its data.

One model for many homes

Problem
Every home's HVAC behaves a bit differently, so models usually need data from each home first.
What I'm doing
Training one model on thousands of simulated homes so it can handle homes it has never seen.
Status
In progress.
Published

An LLM that sees what you're doing

Problem
A thermostat doesn't know if you're asleep, cooking, or working out.
What I did
Had a multimodal LLM read what the person is doing from video and pass that to the controller.
Status
In simulation it cut HVAC electricity by 60% compared with the same controller on default schedules. Published in 2026.
Ongoing

AI agents as building engineers

Problem
Buildings need routine engineering work, and most homes have no engineer.
What I'm doing
Testing whether a general AI agent can do that work on a real house's data.
Status
Early pilot runs, fall 2026.

I also put two small open-source tools on Buildrix, a hub of building-engineering tools for AI agents: one fits heat-pump performance curves, the other runs EnergyPlus co-simulation from Python.

What I'm working on, and the occasional complaint.

  • Real houses, not just simulated ones Oct 28, 2026

    My HVAC model learned everything it knows from simulated houses. At group meeting the feedback was simple: test it on more real ones. Fair.

  • Free brokers are free until Sunday afternoon Oct 25, 2026

    Our two research houses were sending data through a free public MQTT broker. On Sunday it went down for four and a half hours, and so did most of our data.
