Real houses, not just simulated ones

My HVAC model learned everything it knows from simulated houses. At group meeting the feedback was simple: test it on more real ones. Fair.

So I'm rebuilding the training data on top of ResStock homes and pulling in measured data from more real houses. The first 1,000-house trial run went through on the campus cluster last night.
